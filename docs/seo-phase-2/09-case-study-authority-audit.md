## 1. Governance & Evidence Audit Protocol

To maintain maximum E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) credibility and protect against Google Quality Rater / AI penalties:
- **Rule**: All claims are audited strictly against codebase source code (`src/components/DiagnosticCaseTeardowns.tsx`).
- **Data Integrity**: Unsubstantiated numbers or missing data points are flagged directly. No fake metrics are invented.

## 2. Comprehensive Audit of Active Case Studies

### Case Study 1: B2B Enterprise Software
- **Industry**: B2B Enterprise Software (`ACV ₹8,00,000 / year`).
- **Headline**: *Eliminating 18-Hour Lead Latency & Structuring Enterprise Demo Routing*
- **Problem & Baseline Situation**: Heavy ad spend on LinkedIn & Google Ads, but leads manually assigned via weekly spreadsheet syncs. Baseline lead latency: 18 hours. Conversion: 3.2% Demo-to-Opportunity rate. Ad-hoc follow-up.
- **Intervention & System Fix**: Automated instant reply (< 90s), 7-stage WhatsApp + CRM sync, instant qualification triage & calendar booking.
- **Claimed Results**:
  - Lead latency: 18 hours → `< 90 seconds`
  - Conversion: 3.2% → `11.8% Demo-to-Opportunity rate`
  - Impact: `3.6x increase in qualified sales conversations`
- **Timeframe / Duration**: Not explicitly defined in component state.
- **Evidence Classification**: **Documented but incomplete** (Demonstrates specific before/after metrics and system mechanisms, but lacks client brand name, timestamped CRM screenshot proof, or explicit intervention duration due to client NDA formatting).

### Case Study 3: High-Ticket Medical Equipment & Tech
- **Industry**: High-Ticket Medical Equipment & Tech (`B2B Sales Cycle 60 Days`).
- **Headline**: *Unifying Disconnected Ad Campaigns & Sales Rep Follow-Up Protocols*
- **Problem & Baseline Situation**: Ads driven to separate landing pages while sales reps worked in isolated offline CRM. Latency: 24+ hours via email inquiry forms. Conversion: 4.5% Inquiry-to-Qualified-Lead. Max 2 phone calls before giving up.

- **Timeframe / Duration**: Quarterly baseline reference (90 days).
- **Evidence Classification**: **Documented but incomplete** (Strong commercial context and financial pipeline quantification; requires formal client logo or quote to upgrade to Verified First-Party Evidence).

## 4. Case Study Template Standard for Phase 3

To ensure every future case study published achieves **Verified First-Party Evidence** status:
1. **Client Context**: Business type, ACV/deal size, target buyer profile.
2. **Baseline Diagnosis**: Measured latency, lead drop-off rate, initial conversion %.
3. **Architectural Intervention**: Exact system components built (intake, WhatsApp, CRM, scripts).
4. **Timeframe**: Implementation duration and measurement window (e.g., 90-day post-launch audit).
5. **Verified Impact**: Concrete numerical change in latency, conversion rate, and pipeline value.
6. **System Schema**: Visual flow map showing data movement from ad click to closed revenue.
7. **Service Linkage**: Direct link to the exact Naxolutions service page that built the fix.
