# 04 — Question & Query Universe (60 Opportunities Reassessed & Reclassified)

## 1. Governance & Priority Reclassification Protocol

In accordance with Phase 2.1 QA guidelines:
- **Data Integrity**: Search volume, KD, and CPC remain marked as `NOT VERIFIED` (no manufactured scores).
- **Refined Priority Standard**:
  - **P1 (First-Wave Must-Have)**: Reduced from 32 down to **12 true P1 topics** that materially strengthen the topical architecture.
  - **P2 (Supporting System Content)**: **33 topics** providing essential operational depth.
  - **P3 (Lower Urgency / Strategic Depth)**: **15 topics** for long-tail support.
- **Assigned Content Types**: Every opportunity is assigned one of `Pillar`, `Question Article`, `Commercial Guide`, `Service Page`, `Case Study`, `FAQ / Answer Block`, `Existing Page Enhancement`, `Do Not Create`.
