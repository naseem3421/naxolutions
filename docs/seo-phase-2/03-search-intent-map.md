# 03 — Search Intent Map & Funnel Matrix

## 1. Intent Classification Methodology

To ensure Naxolutions captures high-value commercial prospects across their buyer journey, search intent is evaluated across 10 distinct intent types:

1. **Problem-Aware (Informational)**: Prospect knows they have a symptom (e.g., leads aren't calling back) but doesn't know the root cause.
2. **Solution-Aware (Informational / Commercial)**: Prospect knows the category of solution (e.g., instant WhatsApp follow-up) and searches for methods.
3. **Service-Aware (Commercial / Transactional)**: Prospect searches specifically for a consulting service or agency solution.
4. **Commercial Investigation**: Prospect evaluates options, methodologies, benchmarks, or teardowns.
5. **Transactional / Decision**: Prospect is ready to hire a consultant or request a diagnostic call.
6. **Comparison**: Prospect compares two approaches (e.g., website redesign vs conversion system).
7. **Cost / Pricing**: Prospect researches investment models and cost implications.
8. **Local / Regional**: Prospect explicitly looks for Chennai, Tamil Nadu, or Indian business expertise.
9. **Navigational**: Prospect searches directly for Naxolutions or specific proprietary terms (e.g., Revenue Journey Diagnostic).
10. **AEO / Direct Question**: Prospect asks conversational AI (Gemini, Perplexity, ChatGPT) for direct answers.
