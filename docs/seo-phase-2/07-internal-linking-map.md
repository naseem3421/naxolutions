# 07 — Internal Linking Architecture & Flow Map

## 1. Systemic Linking Rules & Flow Topology

Every piece of content created for Naxolutions must adhere to strict internal link flow rules:

```
[ Informational Question / Guide Article ]
       │
       ├── (Contextual Link) ──────────────► [ Topic Pillar Guide ]
       ├── (Service Callout Link) ─────────► [ Commercial Service Page ]
       ├── (Proof & Teardown Link) ────────► [ Case Study / Teardown Page ]
       └── (Primary Conversion CTA) ──────► [ 1:1 Diagnostic Consultation ]
```

### Linking Rules & Standards:
1. **No Artificial Keyword Stuffing**: Anchors must be natural editorial phrases (e.g., "diagnosing lead response latency", "our Lead Conversion Systems architecture", "see the real estate teardown").
2. **Minimum Link Hierarchy per Article**:
   - Exactly **1 Contextual Link** upward to the parent Topic Pillar.
   - Exactly **1 Service Link** to the primary corresponding commercial service page.
   - Exactly **1 Case Study Link** to a relevant before/after teardown on `/case-studies`.
   - Exactly **1 Conversion CTA Link** to `/consultation`.
   - Exactly **2 Lateral Links** to related supporting question articles within the same cluster.
3. **Breadcrumb Integration**: Every article renders structured HTML breadcrumbs (`Home > Blog > Category > Article Title`) linking to category views.
