# 14 — SEO + GEO + AEO Performance Measurement Framework

## 1. Measurement Governance & Data Integrity

To ensure real accountability and avoid vanity metric reporting:
- **Baseline Protocol**: Unmeasured current numbers are explicitly designated as `UNMEASURED BASELINE (To be tracked in GA4 & Google Search Console)`.
- **Primary Data Sources**: Google Search Console (GSC), Google Analytics 4 (GA4 `G-J3HHN5CR4G`), Perplexity / Gemini citation monitoring, direct inbound diagnostic submissions.
