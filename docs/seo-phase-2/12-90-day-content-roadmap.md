# 12 — 90-Day Content Architecture Roadmap (QA Refined)

## 1. Executive Roadmap Overview

This 90-day roadmap outlines the strategic publishing sequence for establishing topical authority across Naxolutions' 5 core content clusters:
- **Month 1 (Authority Foundation)**: Core Topic Pillars & High-Intent Direct-Answer Articles.
- **Month 2 (Cluster Expansion)**: Operational Frameworks & Deep-Dive Diagnostic Guides.
- **Month 3 (Commercial & Regional Alignment)**: Specific Problem Teardowns, Tactical Scripts, and Localized Case Studies.
