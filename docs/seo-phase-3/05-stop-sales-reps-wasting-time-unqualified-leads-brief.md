## CORE PROBLEM
Allowing open, friction-free calendar booking directly from web forms leads to flooded sales calendars filled with unqualified prospects. Sales reps expend high-value diagnostic capacity pitching leads who cannot afford the solution. This leads to rep burnout, drawn-out sales cycles, and missed opportunities with real buyers.

---

## CORE ANSWER / THESIS
To stop sales reps from wasting capacity on unqualified leads, businesses must implement an upfront diagnostic qualification filter. Instead of giving direct calendar access to anyone with an email address, intake systems must evaluate budget range, decision authority, and fit criteria. Only leads meeting minimum thresholds get automated calendar booking; unfit leads are routed to self-service resources.
