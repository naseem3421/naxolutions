## Key Content Highlights

### Article 4: Why Sales Reps Reject Marketing Leads
- **Core Diagnosis**: Reps reject marketing leads due to zero context transfer (not knowing which ad/offer the prospect clicked) and conflicting incentive metrics (CPL vs closed revenue).
- **Core Framework**: 4-step alignment architecture, UTM-to-CRM context injection, and MQL/SQL SLA matrix.
- **Evidence Used**: MedTech case study showing 4.5% to 14.2% qualification increase.

### Article 5: How to Stop Sales Reps Wasting Time on Unqualified Leads
- **Core Diagnosis**: Open, un-gated public calendar links and contact-only web forms flood sales calendars with budget-less prospects, costing teams ~156 wasted rep hours monthly.
- **Core Framework**: 4-point qualification filter (Fit, Authority, Timeline, Budget), budget scripting copy for web forms, and automated 3-tier triage matrix.
- **Evidence Used**: B2B Enterprise Software case study showing 3.2% to 11.8% Demo-to-Opportunity rate improvement (3.6x increase in qualified conversations).

### Article 6: Why Commercial Proposals Stop Responding After Sales Calls
- **Core Diagnosis**: Emailing static PDF proposals cold without securing a live review call transfers all momentum to busy buyers, leading to unaddressed pricing hesitations and radio silence.
- **Core Framework**: 4-stage post-proposal protocol, mandatory live proposal review rule, script comparison table (Weak "check-in" vs Value-driven follow-ups), and Clean Break email template.
- **Evidence Used**: B2B Enterprise Software 7-stage automated multi-channel follow-up sequence.
