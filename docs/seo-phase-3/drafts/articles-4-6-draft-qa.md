## Detailed Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Search Intent & Audience Alignment
- **Article 4**: Targets VPs of Sales and CMOs experiencing internal blame games over ad lead quality. Directly answers *"Why do sales reps reject leads generated by marketing ads?"* with structural diagnostic clarity.
- **Article 5**: Targets Founders and Sales Directors overwhelmed with unqualified calendar bookings. Directly answers *"How to stop sales reps from wasting time on unqualified leads?"* with practical intake filters.
- **Article 6**: Targets Account Executives and Sales Leaders experiencing post-call ghosting. Directly answers *"Why do proposals vanish into radio silence after sales calls?"* with post-proposal follow-up protocols.

### 2. Mandatory Evidence Compliance
- **Zero Inventions**: No fake client names, fictitious quotes, invented testimonials, or unverified statistical claims were added.
- **Documented First-Party Evidence**:
  - Article 4 references the **MedTech Teardown** (`/case-studies#healthcare-medical-tech`) with verified baseline metric: Inquiry-to-Qualified-Lead conversion rate increase from **4.5% to 14.2%**.
  - Article 5 references the **B2B Enterprise SaaS Teardown** (`/case-studies#b2b-saas-enterprise`) with verified baseline metrics: Demo-to-Opportunity rate increase from **3.2% to 11.8%** (3.6x increase in qualified sales conversations).
  - Article 6 references the **B2B Enterprise SaaS Teardown** (`/case-studies#b2b-saas-enterprise`) with verified 7-stage automated multi-channel follow-up sequence.

### 3. E-E-A-T & Practical Usefulness
- **Consultancy Tone**: Each article is written from an authoritative, first-person operational perspective ("Through our diagnostic audits across B2B enterprises...").
- **Actionable Frameworks**:
  - Article 4 provides a 4-step alignment framework, UTM context sync architecture, and MQL/SQL SLA comparison table.
  - Article 5 provides a capacity financial cost calculation table, 4-point qualification filter, web form budget script copy, and automated triage decision matrix.
  - Article 6 provides a 4-stage post-proposal follow-up protocol, live proposal review rule, script comparison table (Weak vs Value-driven), and Clean Break email template.

### 4. SEO, AEO, and GEO Optimization
- **Direct Answer Callout Blocks**: Each article starts with a highlighted `> **DIRECT ANSWER // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**` block (40–80 words) optimized for AI search engines (Perplexity, SearchGPT, Gemini) and Google AI Overviews.
- **Structural Hierarchy**: Clean H1, H2, and H3 progression with descriptive subheadings.
- **ASCII Process Diagrams**: Formatted ASCII diagrams visualize intent decay curves, context pipelines, qualification flows, and follow-up sequences.

### 5. Cannibalization Prevention

- **Article 6** focuses strictly on post-proposal follow-up and review call rules, maintaining clear differentiation from `/services/sales-process-optimization`.
