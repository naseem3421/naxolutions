---

### Publication Gate Protocol

The Publication Gate establishes the mandatory quality and safety criteria required before any Phase 3 article can move from draft status to live production deployment.

To pass the gate, each article must satisfy eight non-negotiable dimensions:
1. **Content**: Accurate problem diagnosis, clear structure, no generic AI filler.
2. **Evidence**: 100% verified against documented baseline audits; zero invented statistics or client identities.
3. **SEO**: Primary query alignment, canonical slug structure, meta description recommendations.
4. **AEO**: Direct answer block present, extractable tables, structured checklists.
5. **GEO**: Clear entity positioning as a Business Conversion Consultancy; natural regional context.
6. **Commercial**: Logical progression from diagnosis to Naxolutions service architecture and diagnostic CTA.
7. **Editorial**: Professional tone, clean prose, zero grammatical errors.
8. **Status**: Overall publication gate decision (`PASS`, `MINOR FIXES`, `MAJOR FIXES`, `HOLD`).

### Detailed Status Definitions

- **PASS**: Meets 100% of technical, evidence, and editorial standards. Approved for production deployment.
- **MINOR FIXES**: Draft content and evidence are fully verified. Requires minor Phase 3E editorial polish (clarifying top-30 word direct answers, expanding technical CRM checklists) before final gate sign-off.
- **MAJOR FIXES**: Structural flaws, unverified evidence, or cannibalization conflicts detected. Requires substantial rewriting before re-evaluation.
- **HOLD**: Article fails core strategic criteria or evidence validation. Publication blocked.
