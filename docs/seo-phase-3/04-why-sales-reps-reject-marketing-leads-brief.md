## CORE PROBLEM
Marketing measures success on lead volume and Cost Per Lead (CPL), while sales measures success on closed revenue. Ad campaigns capture generic curiosity clicks without qualifying intent. When leads hit the CRM, sales reps receive zero context on what ad the prospect clicked, leading to generic sales calls that fail to convert and cause reps to abandon ad leads entirely.

---

## CORE ANSWER / THESIS
Sales reps reject marketing leads because of context loss and misaligned definitions of a "qualified lead". Marketing focuses on click volume, while sales requires commercial intent. To eliminate rep rejection, businesses must implement a unified Marketing-to-Sales System that enforces shared qualification criteria, passes ad click context (UTMs/ad offer) directly into sales rep scripts, and automates lead routing.
