## CORE PROBLEM
Most B2B websites are built as digital brochures focused on aesthetic visual appeal, generic company history, and jargon-heavy descriptions. They fail to clarify the core value proposition in the first 6 seconds, omit buyer objection-handling copy, present friction-heavy intake forms, and fail to provide immediate low-friction communication channels.

---

## CORE ANSWER / THESIS
A website receives traffic without inquiries because of commercial architecture failure, not ad traffic quality. Visitors exit within 8 seconds when messaging is vague, trust proof is hidden, and contact options create high friction. To fix zero-inquiry websites, businesses must transform digital brochures into commercial conversion engines that articulate clear positioning, address buyer objections, and provide frictionless intake channels.

## FAQ PLAN
1. **Question**: *What is a good conversion rate for a B2B service website?*
   - *Why It Belongs*: Establishes industry standards.
   - *Answer Points*: 3% to 7% visitor-to-qualified-enquiry is standard for high-ticket B2B; under 1% indicates severe commercial friction.
2. **Question**: *Should we redesign our website or fix the existing layout?*
   - *Why It Belongs*: Critical business decision.
   - *Answer Points*: Do not rebuild aesthetically; audit and fix positioning copy, trust signals, and form UX first.
3. **Question**: *How many form fields should a B2B contact form have?*
   - *Why It Belongs*: UX optimization query.
   - *Answer Points*: Keep initial intake short (3–4 fields); collect deeper qualification data on step 2 or via automated messaging.
4. **Question**: *Does website speed directly affect lead inquiries?*
   - *Why It Belongs*: Technical factor inquiry.
   - *Answer Points*: Yes, page load delays over 3 seconds increase bounce rates by over 50%, killing inquiries before headlines load.
