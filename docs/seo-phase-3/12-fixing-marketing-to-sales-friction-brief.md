## CORE PROBLEM
Marketing and sales teams operate as disconnected silos. Marketing optimizes for top-of-funnel lead volume, ad CTR, and Cost Per Lead (CPL), while sales reps require qualified buyer intent and context. Because ad click context (UTMs, specific offer) is lost at form submit, reps receive generic leads, conduct unstructured calls, and ultimately abandon ad-generated prospects.

---

## CORE ANSWER / THESIS
Marketing-to-sales friction is the operational and technological gap between acquiring customer attention through advertising and converting that interest into closed revenue. Eliminating this friction requires a Marketing-to-Sales System that enforces shared qualification SLAs, automates instant CRM routing, passes ad intent directly into sales scripts, and measures closed-loop revenue attribution.

## FAQ PLAN
1. **Question**: *What is the cost of marketing-to-sales friction in a B2B company?*
   - *Why It Belongs*: Financial impact query.
   - *Answer Points*: It typically wastes 30% to 50% of paid ad budgets through un-followed leads, lost ad context, and rep rejection.
2. **Question**: *How do you pass UTM data from a web form into a sales rep's CRM call script?*
   - *Why It Belongs*: Technical implementation query.
   - *Answer Points*: Capture hidden form fields (utm_source, utm_campaign, utm_term) during intake and map them to CRM lead record fields rendered on the rep's call view.
3. **Question**: *What is closed-loop revenue attribution?*
   - *Why It Belongs*: Attribution definition.
   - *Answer Points*: A tracking process that connects signed contract revenue in CRM back to the specific marketing channel and ad campaign that initiated the lead.
4. **Question**: *How do you handle conflict between external ad agencies and internal sales teams?*
   - *Why It Belongs*: Organizational governance query.
   - *Answer Points*: Establish a joint MQL/SQL Service Level Agreement (SLA) focused on qualified revenue rather than raw CPL or ad impression metrics.
