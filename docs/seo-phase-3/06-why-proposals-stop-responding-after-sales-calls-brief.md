## CORE PROBLEM
Emailing a proposal into the void without securing a scheduled review meeting or decision milestone destroys deal momentum. The prospect becomes distracted by daily operational fires, hesitates over unaddressed pricing concerns, and avoids responding to awkward "just checking in" emails from sales reps.

---

## CORE ANSWER / THESIS
Proposals vanish into radio silence because the sales call ended without a clear mutual decision agreement. Emailing a static document transfers all momentum to the busy buyer. To eliminate post-proposal ghosting, sales teams must never email a proposal cold—they must review proposals live during a scheduled decision call, establish clear evaluation milestones, and deploy automated multi-channel follow-up sequences.
