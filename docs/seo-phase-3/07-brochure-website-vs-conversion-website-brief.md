## CORE PROBLEM
Traditional web design agencies focus almost exclusively on visual aesthetics, modern animations, color palettes, and stock photography. They treat a website as a digital brochure. As a result, the website lacks clear positioning, fails to address buyer objections, presents friction-heavy intake forms, and leaves visitors confused about what the company actually does—causing high bounce rates and zero inquiries.

---

## CORE ANSWER / THESIS
The commercial difference between a brochure website and a conversion website is strategic purpose. A brochure website displays company information; a conversion website guides qualified prospects through a structured decision pathway. Conversion websites prioritize positioning clarity, buyer objection handling, trust engineering, and low-friction intake infrastructure over pure aesthetic decoration.
