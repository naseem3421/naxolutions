## CORE PROBLEM
When a prospect messages on WhatsApp asking for price, replying with a raw numerical price or static PDF brochure instantly ends the conversation. The prospect gets what they want, evaluates it without commercial context, and moves on to another vendor. Without an active qualification triage framework, 90%+ of inbound WhatsApp chats die at the price inquiry stage.

---

## CORE ANSWER / THESIS
WhatsApp leads ask for price because price is their default anchor when value is unclear. Sending a price quote immediately turns your high-ticket service into a commodity. To convert price queries into calls, businesses must deploy an automated interactive triage flow that acknowledges the pricing request, delivers structured initial value (e.g., brochure + diagnostic menu), and asks 1–2 smart qualification questions before providing customized pricing.
