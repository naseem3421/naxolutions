## CORE PROBLEM
Placing un-gated calendar booking links directly on public websites allows any visitor to claim 30–60 minutes of senior sales rep time. Without mandatory upfront qualification filters (budget, authority, timeline, problem scope), sales teams spend hours on calls discovering the prospect has zero budget or isn't a decision-maker.

---

## CORE ANSWER / THESIS
To qualify B2B leads before booking a call, businesses must replace open calendar links with a gated diagnostic intake workflow. Intending bookers must pass through a 3-step qualification filter evaluating project scope, budget range, and decision authority. Only leads meeting baseline criteria receive calendar booking links; unqualified leads are routed to helpful automated resources.
