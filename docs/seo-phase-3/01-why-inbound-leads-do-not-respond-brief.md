## CORE PROBLEM
Inbound form submissions sit unhandled in email inboxes or manual queues for 3 to 18 hours. During this delay, buyer intent cools by over 80%, and the prospect contacts 2–3 faster-replying competitors. When sales reps finally call, the prospect no longer remembers submitting the form or has already scheduled calls elsewhere.

---

## CORE ANSWER / THESIS
The primary reason form leads do not respond to calls is response latency. Buyer intent peaks at the exact moment of form submission. Every minute of delay creates intent decay. To solve ghosted calls, businesses must eliminate manual inbox queues and deploy automated instant multi-channel intake (WhatsApp/SMS/Email) within 60 seconds of form submission.
