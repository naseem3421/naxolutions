## CORE PROBLEM
Enterprise businesses lose massive revenue because inbound WhatsApp conversations occur on sales reps' un-monitored personal phones. Replies are delayed by hours, price queries get ghosted, lead data is never logged in CRMs, and when sales reps leave the company, client relationship history is completely lost.

---

## CORE ANSWER / THESIS
An Enterprise WhatsApp Sales System is an automated conversational architecture built on the WhatsApp Business API that transforms raw inbound chats into structured, qualified sales pipeline data. It provides sub-60-second automated response, diagnostic chat qualification, multi-agent CRM routing, and complete conversation logging to ensure zero WhatsApp leads leak.
